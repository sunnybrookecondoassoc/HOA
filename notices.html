🚨 Missing Person? (Not a Pet)

If a child, elder, or anyone else is missing, call 911 right away. This website is never the first step — police can also trigger an official statewide alert, which this site cannot.

Learn about New Hampshire’s AMBER Alert & Missing Vulnerable Adult Alert systems →

Once law enforcement is already involved, contact our Association President, Fred Levasseur, with as much as you can: a recent photo, when and where they were last seen, a physical description, and what they were wearing. Fred Levasseur will take it from there — getting it posted here, shared to the Facebook group, and sent out as an urgent push notification.
