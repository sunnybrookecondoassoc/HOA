Just a reminder we have our Sunnybrooke Condo Association owners annual meeting coming up on September 14, 2026. Refreshments will be served beginning at 6:30pm and the meeting at 7:00pm.

The meeting will be held at 1 Cielo Dr, Dover, NH 03820


The annual meeting allows for the opportunity for owners to meet each other, meet members of the board of directors and property management staff, report to the owners on HOA business, and conduct an election for the 2 board of director seats. Listen to the speeches from each of the 5 candidates running for the board.

You should have already received your formal notice, election ballot/proxy and tax resolution by email earlier today. They have also been just put in the mail to owners for regular USPS mail delivery. Please fill out the ballot proxy and mail it in right away. If for some reason you don't receive your ballot proxy by email or regular mail, please contact Kelly Allen, Sunnybrooke Condo Association Secretary, to obtain one. Her email is sunnybrookecondo@gmail.com

If you have questions or subjects you would like the board of directors or property management to address, please email them to Kelly Allen at sunnybrookecondo@gmail.com

We look forward to seeing you on September 14th!
